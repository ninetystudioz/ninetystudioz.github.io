1. Who we are

The Ninety app is the internal work system of Ninety Studioz (architecture, interior design and visualization studio) and Ninety Fabrication (manufacturing), Fifth Settlement, New Cairo, Egypt. The app is published by Mahmoud Fawzy. Contact: info@ninetystudioz.com · +20 111 454 5017.

The app is for our employees and authorized team members only. Accounts are created by the company; the public cannot sign up.

2. Data we process

  • Account and work profile: name, work phone number (used as the login), optional personal e-mail (for activation and password reset codes), job title, department, role and profile photo.
  • Attendance: check-in / check-out times, breaks, overtime, and the location of the phone only at the moment you punch (to confirm you are at the workplace or at an assigned errand location). The app does not track location in the background.
  • Device verification: when you enable fingerprint / face verification, your biometric data never leaves your phone; only a device credential (public key) is stored to confirm it is your registered device.
  • Work content: tasks, approvals, requests (leave, permissions, advances), projects, notes, chat messages, photos and files you add as proof of work.
  • Calls: voice/video calls between team members are connected directly between devices; we do not record calls. Technical call-quality data (connection type, duration) may be logged.
  • HR and payroll: for authorized HR/accounts staff only — salary, deductions and payroll records.
  • Notifications and security logs: a push-notification token for your device, device type, IP address and an audit log of sign-ins and changes, used to secure accounts.
  • Office door (Studioz): door-open requests (who, when, from which location) when you use the in-app door button or NFC sticker.

3. Device permissions

  • Location — only when you punch in/out or confirm arrival at an errand.
  • Camera / photos — to take or attach work photos, receipts and profile pictures.
  • Microphone — for in-app calls and voice notes.
  • Notifications — tasks, approvals and reminders.
  • NFC — to read the office door sticker (Studioz).

Each permission is requested only when the related feature is used and can be turned off from your phone settings.

4. How we use the data

Only to run the company's internal operations: attendance and payroll, task and project management, approvals, internal communication, and account security. We do not sell data, use it for advertising, or share it with advertisers.

5. Who can see it

Access inside the app is role-based (for example, only HR/accounts can see payroll; the owner sees management reports). Data is stored with our infrastructure providers (Supabase for the database and authentication, and cloud file storage for uploaded files), which process it on our behalf, encrypted in transit (HTTPS). We disclose data only if required by law.

6. Retention

Work and attendance records are kept for as long as needed for employment, payroll and legal record-keeping. When employment ends, the account is deactivated and access is removed immediately.

7. Your rights and account deletion

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data. To request deletion of your account and associated data, follow the steps in Account & data deletion. Some records (for example payroll and attendance) may be retained for the period required by Egyptian labour and tax law, then deleted.

8. Children

The app is not intended for children and is used only by adult employees.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.

Account & data deletion — حذف الحساب والبيانات

To delete your Ninety account and the personal data linked to it:

  1. Send an e-mail to info@ninetystudioz.com with the subject “Ninety account deletion”, from your registered e-mail, or ask your HR manager.
  2. Include your full name and your registered mobile number.
  3. We confirm your identity, deactivate the account immediately and delete your profile, photos, device credentials, notification tokens and chat content within 30 days. Attendance and payroll records required by law are kept only for the legal period, then deleted.
